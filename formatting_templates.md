# Formatting Templates

These templates are structural and typographic interpretations of the resume
samples on PDF pages 34, 36, and 37. They standardize the layout while
preserving the user's content.

## Page 34 - Master's / Standard

Use when the resume is early-career, master's-level, or not primarily a
research record.

- Clean sans-serif type.
- Name and one-line contact header.
- Uppercase section headings with a thin rule.
- Main order: Education, Experience, Projects, Skills.
- Entry header: organization/location on the left; role/dates on the right.
- Bullets for experience, research, teaching, and projects.
- Skills grouped into short category lines.
- One page preferred.

## Page 36 - PhD / Research

Use when the resume is a formal academic/research record with publications,
fellowships, teaching, and service.

- Serif type for a formal research feel.
- Name and contact header.
- Title-case section headings with a rule.
- Main order: Education, Experience, Research, Teaching,
  Fellowships and Awards, Professional and Public Service,
  Publications and Conferences.
- Research entries show question/context, method, and outcome/publication.
- Awards include selectivity or context.
- Two pages are acceptable.

## Page 37 - Technical / PhD

Use for a technical research resume that benefits from a summary and a clear
skills block.

- Sans-serif type.
- Name, contact header, and a short Summary.
- Uppercase section headings with a rule.
- Main order: Summary, Education, Skills, Experience, Research, Teaching,
  Projects, Honors and Awards, Publications, Activities.
- Skills appear before experience when they are central to the target role.
- Publications and activities are separated cleanly.
- Two pages are acceptable.

## Shared rules

- Consistent margins, font sizes, and section spacing.
- Organization/location left; role/dates right where possible.
- Bullets are short and start with an action verb.
- No text boxes or decorative graphics in the main ATS version.
- Dates use one format throughout.
- The formatter reformats the supplied content; it does not invent facts.
